# Trademark policy

The code and the character designs of Aora Bot are [MIT](./LICENSE) licensed. The
MIT License covers copyright only. The name "Aora Bot" and the Aora Bot logo identify
the official project, and this policy sets out how they may be used. It exists so that
people can tell the official packages and site from forks and derived products, not to
limit what you do with the code.

## Fine without asking

- Saying truthfully that your product, article, tutorial, talk or course uses or is
  about Aora Bot, for example "Powered by Aora Bot", "built with Aora Bot" or
  "a theme for Aora Bot".
- Redistributing the official, unmodified packages under their own names.
- Using the name for a community group, meetup or chat, as long as it is clear that it
  is run by the community and not by the project.

## Needs written permission

- Naming a fork, a modified version, a product, a package, a company or a domain
  "Aora Bot", or something close enough to be confused with it, such as
  "Aora Bot Pro" or "AoraBot UI".
- Publishing packages under the `@aora-bot` npm scope, or under names that suggest
  they are official.
- Using the Aora Bot logo as the logo or icon of your own product.
- Anything that suggests the project endorses, sponsors or partners with you.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. Give yours its own name and logo, and say that it is
"based on Aora Bot" with a link to this repository.

# 商标政策

Aora Bot 的代码和角色设计以 [MIT 许可证](./LICENSE)开源，但 MIT 许可证只涉及著作权。「Aora Bot」名称和标志用于标识官方项目，本政策说明它们可以如何使用。这样做是为了让大家分清哪些是官方的包和网站，哪些是 fork 和衍生产品，而不是限制你如何使用代码。

## 无需申请

- 如实说明你的产品、文章、教程、演讲或课程使用了 Aora Bot，或以 Aora Bot 为主题，例如「Powered by Aora Bot」「基于 Aora Bot 构建」「Aora Bot 主题」。
- 以原有名称再分发未经修改的官方包。
- 用这个名称建立社区交流群、线下活动或聊天群，但要说明是社区自发组织，并非项目官方。

## 需要书面许可

- 把 fork、修改版、产品、包、公司或域名命名为「Aora Bot」，或使用容易与之混淆的名称，例如「Aora Bot Pro」「AoraBot UI」。
- 在 `@aora-bot` npm 作用域下发布包，或使用让人误以为是官方的包名。
- 把 Aora Bot 标志用作你自己产品的标志或图标。
- 任何暗示本项目为你背书、赞助或与你合作的用法。

## 关于 fork

欢迎 fork。请给你的项目起自己的名称、使用自己的标志，并注明「基于 Aora Bot」，附上本仓库链接。

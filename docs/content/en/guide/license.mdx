## Credit

When a free user publicly releases or launches a product that uses Aora Bot, it must credit Aora Bot in any one of these places:

- The product's About, Credits, Help or settings page
- The website footer
- The product description in an app store, a plugin marketplace or on the project's homepage
- The product's README, documentation or third-party license list
- For a product that cannot carry a link, such as a hardware device, offline software or a game: its manual, packaging, end credits or staff list, with the full URL written out

The credit must include the words "Aora Bot" and link to the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/sam70361/aora-bot); once an official website launches, it may link there instead. It need not appear next to the characters. For example:

```html
Powered by <a href="https://github.com/sam70361/aora-bot">Aora Bot</a>
```

Personal use, learning and research, internal testing and projects not yet publicly released need no credit. Organisations holding a commercial license may omit it.

## Restrictions

The following restrictions apply to all users, commercial licensees included, unless otherwise agreed in writing:

- Selling, renting or charging for the engine or the characters as a product of their own, for example as a component library, template, UI kit, source bundle, SDK or plugin.
- Offering a hosted service or API whose main value is Aora Bot, such as a character, avatar or animated-expression service.
- Selling the characters as goods of their own, including stickers, sticker packs, merchandise, prints and digital collectibles, or selling exported images, animations and asset packs on their own.
- Registering a character's name or appearance as a trademark, or claiming copyright in it.
- Training or fine-tuning AI models that generate characters on the character designs.
- Removing or altering the copyright and license notices in the source.

Using a character as an avatar, assistant or mascot in your own product is not subject to these restrictions.

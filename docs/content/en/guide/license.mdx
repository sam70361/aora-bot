Most people use it free, in commercial projects too; larger companies need a commercial license once they ship it. This page puts it in plain words; the license text is what counts.

## Free use

Any one of these is enough to use Aora Bot free, commercial use included:

| Who                                    | Notes                                                            |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Individuals and independent developers | Using it in your own name, including in your own products        |
| Students, teachers and schools         | For teaching, learning and research                              |
| Non-profits                            | Including charitable projects and community groups               |
| Open-source projects                   | Projects that publish all their source and do not run for profit |
| Teams of up to 3                       | Full-time, part-time and long-term contracted staff, 3 in total  |

Free users can build Aora Bot into websites, apps, games and hardware, change it, and ship it with their products. No attribution is needed in your interface.

When you build for someone else, what counts is who ultimately owns or runs the product. A freelancer building for a 50-person company needs that company to hold a commercial license.

## Commercial license

Organisations that fit none of the above, such as companies of more than 3 people, need a commercial license to use Aora Bot in a live product or service. Until then, evaluation, internal testing and prototyping are free.

Commercial licenses come as a yearly subscription or a perpetual license for a version, with scope, term and fee agreed case by case. These apply to everyone, commercial licensees included, unless agreed otherwise in writing:

- Selling, renting or charging for the engine or the characters as a product of their own, for example as a component library, template, UI kit, source bundle, SDK or plugin.
- Offering a hosted service or API whose main value is Aora Bot, such as a character, avatar or animated-expression service.
- Selling the characters as goods of their own, including stickers, sticker packs, merchandise, prints and digital collectibles, or selling exported images, animations and asset packs on their own.
- Registering a character's name or appearance as a trademark, or claiming copyright in it.
- Training or fine-tuning AI models that generate characters on the character designs.
- Removing or altering the copyright and license notices in the source.

Using a character as an avatar, assistant or mascot in your own product is not affected by any of these.

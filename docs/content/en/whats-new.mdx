# What's New

The changes in each release, newest first. Every package, export and parameter change is listed in full in the [changelog](https://github.com/sam70361/aora-bot/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## 0.2.0

This release has breaking changes; read "Breaking changes" below before upgrading.

### Characters and faces

- 25 new characters, each with a cast member, including a `treat` family, an `object` family and a `coal` family; `ghost` is redrawn rounder. The coal family is three black lumps with uneven edges: Cinder (`chunk`), all blunt corners, Sooty (`lump`), round and bumpy all the way round, and Ashy (`fluff`), a tuft of soot bristling all the way round.
- 13 new drawn faces. `capsule` now comes first in the face list and pickers. `teardrop` is now a tall dark drop with one highlight, and `specs` small, bold round glasses with a flat bridge and one dark eye with a highlight in each lens. Each drawn face covers all 44 states and works in every vector variant, in pixel and in kaomoji. `defineFace` registers a face of your own.
- Dot (`dot`, 球球) is a cream-white ball wearing `capsule` and now comes first in the cast.

### Styles

- Six drawing media: `pencil`, `marker`, `crayon`, `watercolor`, `neon` and `chalk`.
- Two paper media: `origami`, a sheet crumpled and pressed flat into soft facets, and `relief`, a thick card pressed up from behind. See [Vector styles](/design/styles/vector).
- `gothic` is the sixteenth vector variant: each piece darkened from the edge in, hatched and cross-hatched in ink on the side away from the light, and ringed by a brushed ink line that is heavier in shadow, over a paper grain.
- `defineVectorMedium` registers a vector variant of your own.
- A shape part can carry its own colour, and `defineShapeWithProps` dresses a character in props.

### Colours

- Cream (`cream`) is listed and comes first in the colour list: it is Dot's colour. Ivory (`ivory`), the palette's white, follows it. Both take a one-pixel edge on a light page, so they never melt into it.
- Four muted colours: `sage`, `clay`, `navy` and `mauve`, each tuned for a light and a dark page.
- Avatars keep the colour they had: an id is hashed onto a fixed list of colours (`ASSIGNABLE_COLOR_IDS`), so new colours never move an existing avatar.
- Light and very dark colours read on either page: outlines, kaomoji and the line face are deepened on a light page, keeping their hue, and lifted on a dark one; cream's cut-out eyes turn dark and night's white; in riso cream is no longer covered by pink; on the LED panel black lights white. Picker chips show the colour on the current page, so black is white in dark mode, and a faint chip gets a ring.

### Website

- The home page has two views. The console is where you build a character. The exhibit steps through the cast and every state, with autoplay and an attribute panel for shape, colour, face, style and parameters. The view is remembered, and `?view=exhibit` links to it.
- The wall of characters behind the home page is drawn as line art by default; a switch turns it back to colour.
- The site opens in dark mode on a first visit. The switch is still there, and your choice is remembered.
- The home page's characters wear a soft light: the body rolls into shade at the lower right and catches a thin rim of light at the upper left, while the faces and effects stay flat. The console's thumbnail strips now use the same face colours as the big character.

### Themes

- Seven holiday and seasonal themes with 122 characters between them: Halloween, Christmas, Spring Festival, Mid-Autumn, Easter, Summer and Valentine's Day.
- Themes are separate entries (`@aora-bot/engine/themes/<id>`) that are loaded only when needed, each with its own characters, cast, skins and labels. The [Themes](/themes/) page shows every theme.

### Breaking changes

- Shape space is now 240 units (`SHAPE_SPACE`), with a `-24 -24 288 288` view box. Custom outlines are normalised automatically.
- `FaceTune` fields are ratios of the fitted face: 1 means as fitted.
- React and surfaces components have new names: for example, `CharacterPicker` is now `CharacterSelector`, `AvatarStack` is `AvatarGroup`, `AgentAvatar` is `AgentPortrait` and `CastStage` is `CastLineup`. The changelog lists every rename.
- Characters default to 32 px in React and in `<aora-bot>`.
- Confetti and ribbons are redesigned, with a new palette.

## 0.1.3

- All four packages are released under the Aora Bot License 1.1. Once a product that uses Aora Bot is publicly released, free users credit it once. The code is unchanged.
- A new [Copyright](/guide/copyright) page, and a copyright notice at the end of every page.

## 0.1.2

- `<aora-bot>` accepts `style-options`, a JSON object of style parameters.
- The avatar endpoint accepts `shape`, `color`, `face` and each style parameter by name.
- The console can copy a character as React code, an `<aora-bot>` tag or an avatar link.

## 0.1.1

- First release: the engine, React components, the `<aora-bot>` Web Component, product surfaces, the avatar endpoint and this documentation site.
- 46 characters, three faces, 44 animation states, and three style series: vector, pixel and kaomoji.
